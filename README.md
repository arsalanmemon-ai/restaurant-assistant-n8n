# restaurant-assistant-n8n
AI-powered Restaurant Assistant built with n8n and Google Sheets. It answers FAQs, shows the menu, and records orders automatically.
# Google Sheet Restaurant Management  
**AI + Google Sheets = Restaurant Assistant**

This project connects Google Gemini AI with Google Sheets to manage restaurant operations smartly.

### 🔹 Features:
- AI understands customer questions
- Menu & FAQs are fetched from Google Sheets
- Orders are automatically stored in the sheet
- Smart instant replies are generated by AI

### 💡 Use Case:
Perfect for restaurants, cafes, and food delivery services to automate chat-based ordering and customer support.
